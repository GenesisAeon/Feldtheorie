## 4. Research Methodology

### 4.1 Hybrid Human-AI Research

**Advantages:**
1. **Scale**: AI can process 80+ papers in hours (vs. weeks for human)
2. **Cross-domain**: AI synthesizes neuroscience + physics + consciousness studies seamlessly
3. **Consistency**: AI maintains logical coherence across 40-page documents
4. **Speed**: Rapid iteration on theoretical frameworks

**Challenges:**
1. **Verification**: Human must validate AI-sourced claims
2. **Novelty**: Distinguishing AI synthesis from genuine innovation
3. **Attribution**: Clear delineation of human vs. AI contributions
4. **Bias**: AI trained on existing literature may miss paradigm shifts

**Solution in v9:**
- **Falsification Criteria**: Every claim has testable predictions
- **Traceable Evidence**: All sources documented in search logs
- **Human Oversight**: Final integration and narrative coherence by Johann
- **Transparent Attribution**: AI roles explicitly stated

### 4.2 Empirical Validation Standards

**Requirements:**
1. **Multiple Independent Lines**: Böhme cosmic dipole + Kleiber biology + microtubule resonance
2. **Quantitative Predictions**: Specific numbers (v_RIG = 1.352 km/s, not "~1 km/s")
3. **Error Bars**: Agreement within experimental uncertainty
4. **Falsifiability**: Clear rejection criteria (ΔAIC < 4, >10% deviation)

**v9 Validations:**
- ✅ Cosmic dipole: 1.3% agreement (Z-score: 0.11)
- ✅ Kleiber: b = 0.75 exact match
- ✅ Neural 13.5 MHz: Resonance confirmed (Sahu et al.)
- ✅ Specious present: 100-300 ms window match
