## 🚀 THE FRONTIER: Three-Dimensional Scaling

We scale in **three dimensions simultaneously**:

### 1. 🗺️ **SPATIAL DIMENSION** (From Lists to Topology)

**V2.0:** Agents existed in abstract space — simple Python lists with no geometry.

**V3.0:** Agents inhabit **real spatial topologies**:
- **Hex Grids** (crystalline lattices for emergent patterns)
- **Graph Networks** (social/trade/information networks)
- **Proximity-Based Interactions** (local neighborhoods vs. global broadcasts)

**Goal:** Emergent spatial structures (cities, trade routes, territories)

---

### 2. 🧠 **COGNITIVE DIMENSION** (From Numbers to Minds)

**V2.0:** Agent cognition = simple scalar values (`health`, `energy`, `age`)

**V3.0:** Agents possess **neural embeddings** (vector minds):
- **Semantic Memory** (256D embedding vectors representing beliefs/knowledge)
- **Cultural Transmission** (memes spread through social graphs)
- **Cognitive Distance** (semantic similarity ≠ spatial proximity)

**Goal:** Emergent cultures, belief systems, and ideologies

---

### 3. ⏳ **TEMPORAL DIMENSION** (From Generations to Civilizations)

**V2.0:** Simulation runs for ~100-200 timesteps (2-3 generations)

**V3.0:** **Epic timescales**:
- **Epochs** (1000+ timesteps)
- **Civilizational Memory** (historical records, technological trees)
- **Long-Term Evolution** (species branching, mass extinctions, renaissances)

**Goal:** Observe the full arc of civilization — rise, golden age, decline, rebirth
