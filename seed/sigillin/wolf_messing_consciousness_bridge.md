## Core Concept

**Wolf Messing** (1899-1974) was a Soviet mentalist who reported telepathy, hypnosis, and "spontaneous precognition" in his memoirs *О самом себе* (1965). While historically contested and politically instrumentalized, his **subjective descriptions** map remarkably onto the v_RIG consciousness framework:

- **v_RIG:** "Plötzlichkeit seiner Eingebungen" (instantaneity) → rapid information integration
- **κ-coupling:** "Gehirn zu Gehirn Übertragung" → inter-brain synchronization
- **β-logistic:** Abrupt consciousness state transitions (hypnotic stages)
- **Sigillin-states:** Non-verbal meaning transfer (mental images, resonance)

**This Sigillin** establishes a methodological bridge: **anecdotal phenomena → operationalized hypotheses → falsifiable experiments**.

## Critical Considerations

### Skepticism & Limitations

⚠️ **Historical Context:**
- Many of Messing's claims are **unverified** (no KGB archives, Stalin meeting contested)
- Einstein-Freud meeting **chronologically impossible** (they met in 1927, after Messing's Indian tour)
- **Political instrumentalization** in Soviet propaganda

⚠️ **Methodological Requirements:**
- **Strict double-blind controls**
- **High statistical power** (psi effects notoriously small)
- **Pre-registration** and **multi-lab replication**
- **Quantitative measures:** EEG, fMRI, GSR, heart rate

### Falsification Criteria

✅ **v8.0 is falsified if:**
1. **No EEG synchronization** above baseline → κ-coupling rejected
2. **No RT advantages** in claimed "mediums" → v_RIG enhancement rejected
3. **No presentiment effects** in controlled settings → precognition hypothesis rejected
4. **Linear hypnosis depth** (no threshold) → β-logistic model rejected
