## 🌌 Philosophical Themes

### Threshold Thinking
Phase transitions aren't just mathematical—they're a **way of seeing the world**:
- Gradual accumulation → sudden transformation
- Quantitative changes → qualitative shifts
- Pattern recognition across scales (neurons, cities, climate)

### Emergence & Integration
- **Emergence:** How does complexity arise from simplicity?
- **Integration:** Can one framework bridge physics, AI, biology, climate?
- **Humility:** What are the limits of universal models?

### Responsibility
With great modeling power comes great responsibility:
- Climate tipping points: informing policy without alarmism
- AI capabilities: understanding without accelerating risk
- Dual-use: science open, but mindful of misuse

The `interpretive/` folder is reserved for:
- **Poetic Reflections** - Artistic responses to science
- **Philosophical Essays** - Deep dives into meaning
- **Narrative Threads** - Story-driven explanations
- **Metaphors** - Bridging math to human experience

**Status:** Planned for future releases

**Principle:** Poetic writing **enhances** understanding but **never replaces** empirical evidence. It lives here, separate from scientific claims.

## 🧩 Sigillin Integration

All narrative documents follow the **Sigillin Trilayer System**:
1. **Formal Thread:** Structured protocols & procedures
2. **Empirical Thread:** Lessons from project experience
3. **Poetic Thread:** Meaning-making & vision

This architecture ensures:
- **Clarity:** Technical specs are precise
- **Context:** Real-world grounding
- **Resonance:** Emotional & philosophical depth
