# Phaethon/Bennu Research Project

## Overview

This research project investigates plasma-driven chimera states in near-Sun asteroids, focusing on Bennu and Phaethon dust emission mechanisms. The work connects frustrated system physics, plasma resonance, and observable phenomena to develop testable predictions for upcoming missions like JAXA's DESTINY+.

## Research Highlights

### Chimera States on Bennu
- Particle ejections show LST (Local Solar Time) clustering at 15-18h
- Frustrated system with jammed/unjammed coexistence
- Thermal, electrical, and oscillatory triggers
- Testable by OSIRIS-REx data analysis

### Phaethon Plasma Resonance
- Alfvén wave-regolith acoustic coupling
- Parker Solar Probe frequency data integration
- KdV soliton modeling for dusty plasma
- DESTINY+ mission predictions

### Geminid Velocity Puzzle
- Observed 35 km/s entry velocity vs. expected orbital mechanics
- Hypothesis: Plasma slow-wave deceleration in solar wind
- MHD resonance and whistler-mode wave coupling
- Cross-validation with radar observations
